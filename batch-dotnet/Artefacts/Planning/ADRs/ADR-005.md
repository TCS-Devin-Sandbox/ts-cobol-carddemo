# ADR-005: Restartability: step state, chunk checkpoints, batch run ledger

Status: Accepted

Every step is appended to `ledger/batch_run_ledger.jsonl` (run id, job, step, program, RC, status, counts). An abended job stores its committed steps and pinned GDG generations in `state/<JOB>.json`; `--restart` skips them. POSTTRAN commits KSDS changes and rejects every `ChunkSize` records with a checkpoint (position, counters, SYSOUT) and resumes there. INTCALC/TRANREPT commit at step end, so a rerun is idempotent.
